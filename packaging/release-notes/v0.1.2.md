Web pages are now local and offline: docview only shows HTML files from your computer, and they can't reach the internet.

## Changed

- **Web pages can't use the network.** Remote images, scripts, fonts, requests and websockets are blocked, so a page can't download anything or send anything out.
- **Links to websites open in your browser,** and so does `docview https://…`. Only local files open in docview.
- **Fewer ways in.** WebRTC, camera and microphone, WebGL, web audio, DRM, clipboard access and pop-ups are off for web pages, and requests for your location or notifications are always refused.
